Overview

Purpose

Pregnancy Stages (for Dads) is designed to give partners a clear, easy-to-follow guide through pregnancy. It explains the biological changes in straightforward language and offers practical, stage-by-stage advice on how to support a partner and prepare for the arrival of a baby. By focusing on the partner’s role, the site addresses a gap often overlooked in traditional medical resources.

Audience

First-Time Fathers: Men navigating pregnancy for the first time who may feel uncertain about their role during the 40-week journey. Supportive Partners: Individuals seeking practical and emotional strategies to better support their pregnant partner. Busy Dads: Those with limited time who prefer concise, actionable guidance—like quick tips and to-do lists—over lengthy medical articles.
